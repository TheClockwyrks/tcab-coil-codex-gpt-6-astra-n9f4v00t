# Coil

One bright serpent. One amber target. A whole board of bad decisions waiting to
happen. Coil is a crisp, neon take on the grid-snake arcade game: easy to start,
hard to put down after a run ends one turn too soon.

Your snake never stops. Steer around the wall, eat a pellet, and leave one more
cell of yourself behind. Every meal changes the route home. Keep your head clear
of your own trail, and leave enough room for the next corner.

Speed of thought is where the score lives. Pick up another pellet before the
combo bar empties and climb toward x5. Take the long way around and your
multiplier drops back to one. The best run balances a hungry shortcut with an
escape route.

Play with arrows or WASD, or use the touch pad. Pause whenever you need a breath,
then chase the session's best score—or stay alive long enough to fill the board.
